Navigating the 30^42 Chemical Search Space via Single-Mod Screening, SPOS Filtering, and Multi-Objective Pareto Optimization

Figure 2 | Combinatorial Beam Search and Chemical Optimization Flowchart. Algorithmic execution pipeline for searching the astronomical 42-position chemical modification state space ($30^{42}$ configurations). Phase 1 (Permutation Scan): A candidate unmodified 21-mer siRNA duplex is exhaustively screened across all 42 positions with available modifications (812 single variants evaluated in < 0.1 s) to establish position-specific delta potency. A pairing pool is assembled ensuring complete 42-position duplex coverage and incorporating the Alnylam ESC+ benchmark ((S)-GNA at antisense position 7). The initial beam is seeded with $W = 20$ diverse candidates. Phase 2 (Combinatorial Beam Expansion): At each expansion round ($D = 1 \dots 8$), child variants are generated and evaluated at >12,000 variants/sec using the Unified 517-D CatBoost Regressor. Concurrently, Solid-Phase Oligonucleotide Synthesis (SPOS) constraints and biophysical gates filter out G-quadruplexes, consecutive bulky LNA blocks, and catalytic slicing window violations. Surviving variants are pruned to the top $W = 20$ until convergence ($\Delta\text{Score} < 0.05$ or max depth). Phase 3 (Pareto Selection): Surviving candidates are evaluated across multi-dose titrations with analytical closed-form Hill inversion ($p\text{IC}_{50}$, $\text{IC}_{50}$) and ranked along a tri-objective Pareto frontier (in vitro knockdown efficacy vs. serum nuclease stability vs. solid-phase synthesis cost), outputting the top 3 clinical lead configurations with 3D PDB models and exportable synthesis budget dossiers.
