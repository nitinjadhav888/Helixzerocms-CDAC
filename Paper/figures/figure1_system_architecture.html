Figure 1 | The Unified HelixZero Computational Architecture. End-to-end data flow illustrating the verified production platform for therapeutic siRNA screening and rational chemical optimization. Stage 1 (Sequence Prior): High-throughput sliding-window dissection of full-length mRNA transcripts (e.g., human TTR, PCSK9), scoring naked 21-mers via Model A (LightGBM GBDT, 214 features, >3,700 candidates/sec; Takayuki $r = 0.8788$, Mixset $r = 0.8291$, Huesken $r = 0.8044$). Negative control evaluation on modified CMsiRNAdb confirms sequence models collapse ($r = 0.1771$), demonstrating the absolute necessity of chemistry-aware modeling. Stage 2 (517-D Multi-Modal Feature Vector): 444 positional chemistry descriptors ($42 \times 10$ physical flags + 24 global structural indicators), 64 evolutionary transformer embeddings from RNA-FM (`rna_fm_t12`), 5 ViennaRNA 2.6 thermodynamic constants, and 4 dynamic dosage covariates ($\log_{10}(\text{Dose\_nM})$, relative ratio, incubation time, hepatic lineage flag). Stage 3 (Unified ML Decision Core): A unified, dose-conditioned CatBoost decision tree regressor (517-D) trained under strict 5-fold sequence GroupKFold ($N = 17,761$ across 5,251 disjoint sequence clusters; $r = 0.6776$, $R^2 = 0.4497$) and validated on held-out multi-dose titrations (Homogeneous $r = 0.8359$, Heterogeneous $r = 0.8334$; ROC-AUC $\ge 0.929$). Evaluates intrinsic affinity analytically via closed-form Hill equation inversion ($p\text{IC}_{50}$ and $\text{IC}_{50}$ in <1 μs). Stage 4 (Guardrails & Translational Handoff): Real-time filtering through 4 deterministic biophysical penalty domains (helicase unwinding barrier, Alnylam AT3 exonuclease shield, TLR7/8 immune masking, and Janas 4,096-hexamer seed cytotoxicity) and a 2-bit SIMD transcriptome-wide off-target firewall (<12 ms). Emits continuous A-form 3D PDB models (B-factor modification encoding), validated with 100% sensitivity across all six commercial FDA-approved therapeutics, and exports wet-lab synthesis budget dossiers.
